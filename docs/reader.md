# The reader API

What the reader needs comes from the server; the browser never contacts a site.

- **Pages.** `GET /api/v1/chapters/{id}/pages` lists a chapter's pages from its chosen copy, with the previous and next chapters in reading order and your progress. Pass `variant_id` to read another copy. When the chosen copy is on a source you may not use, the first copy you may use is read instead. Page images (`…/pages/{index}`) are fetched through the source's plugin within the source's **image** budget, apart from its other calls: the image rate and the number of images at once the plugin declares (no pacing when it declares no image rate), or the assigned network profile's limits. They may be cached by the browser for a day. Page lists are kept for 15 minutes. Downloaded chapters will be served from their files.
- **Progress** is saved per user and chapter (`PUT /api/v1/chapters/{id}/progress`). Reaching the last page marks the chapter read. Your other devices receive a `progress.synced` event. `GET /api/v1/works/{id}/progress` says where to continue: the chapter you read last if unfinished, else the first unread chapter after it. Chapters can be marked read or unread in bulk.
- **Bookmarks** are per user: bookmark one chapter or many at once, or remove them (`POST /api/v1/works/{id}/bookmarks`). Chapters say whether you bookmarked them, the chapter list can show only bookmarked ones (`?bookmarked=true`), and library entries count them. Your other devices receive a `bookmarks.changed` event.
- **Library entries** carry your reading of each work: its chapters, how many you haven't finished (the cover badge), how many are downloaded, whether you started it, when you last read in it, when its newest chapter came out, and the chapter "Continue" opens (the same rule as the work's progress), so the library can filter, sort and open the reader without loading each work.
- **History** lists the chapters you read, newest first, each with when you read it (`GET /api/v1/me/history`): every chapter, or one entry per series (`per=series`: the latest chapter read and where to resume), one series, or series by title (`q`). Taking a series out of your history (`DELETE /api/v1/me/history/{work_id}`) or clearing it (`DELETE /api/v1/me/history`) hides what you read up to now without changing read marks; what you read next shows again. Your history is never shown to anyone else.
- **Reader settings** live at three levels: global, per source and per work. A more specific level's keys win. The server stores them as JSON objects (up to 16 KiB) and doesn't interpret them: the web app owns their shape (zoom, fit, mode, direction, …).
- **Preferences** (theme, colours, fonts) are stored per user the same way and follow you across devices.
